    How to use this timeline

    Explore

    Drag the timeline to travel. On a phone, one finger drags and two fingers pinch to zoom. The buttons on the map zoom in, zoom out, and show everything from Creation to today. Zooming in reveals more people, places, and events. You can also jump with the period buttons or search for a name.

    Three kinds of markers

    Scripture — the Bible tells this. Historical — records from history tell this. Interpretation — a careful idea about a date or a connection. Purple diamonds are never presented as settled fact.

    Some markers carry two badges. The fall of Samaria, for example, is in the Bible and in Assyrian records.

    How dates work

    The line is not an equal number of pixels per year. Ancient centuries sit closer together so Creation and today share one map. Zoom in and the years spread apart. The note under the period buttons tells you how many years are on screen.

    Creation is placed at 4004 BC and the Flood at 2348 BC. Those are James Ussher’s traditional dates, read from the Genesis genealogies. The Bible gives the story and the generations. It does not print those BC numbers.

    From Abraham to the Exodus, this map uses a common conservative chronology with the Exodus in 1446 BC, counting 480 years before Solomon’s temple from 1 Kings 6:1. A separate purple marker shows the other common idea: an Exodus near 1250 BC.

    Where a textbook date would put Egypt’s pyramids before this map’s Flood, the pyramid marker stays after the Flood and the panel tells the textbook date. That placement is labeled Interpretation.

    From the kings of Israel onward, most dates match ordinary history books, such as 722, 701, and 586 BC.

    Parent tools

    Filter books by a child’s age, reading level, fiction or nonfiction, period, and topic. Reading path lines those books up in timeline order. It is a path, not a dump of every title. Notes flag war, slavery, myths, and older attitudes. This library leaves out evolution taught as fact, and it leaves out vulgar or sexual material.

    Keyboard

    Arrow keys move. Plus and minus zoom. Home shows the whole timeline. Tab reaches markers. Enter opens one. Escape closes the panel or this help.
